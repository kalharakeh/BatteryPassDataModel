What changes

  • One line per event in plain English: who, what, which record, and the change itself when it is short ("500 kg → 600 kg"). About 15 events fit on a laptop screen instead of 3–4 today.
  • Grouped by day, newest first, 50 at a time with "Show 50 more".
  • Select a line to open it: who, when (your time and UTC), where it came from, the record with a link to it, and each value before and after with its result. The raw entry stays under "Technical details" for support.
  • Red only for refusals (a refused value, a refused download); every other event has a small grey icon for its kind.
  • Simpler filters: search, record, who and dates in one row; the rarer ones under "More filters".
  • Underneath: a change's audit entry will also record the new value (today it keeps only the old one), and the page looks up record and value names, so lines read "battery mass of model Universal", not IDs. Older events keep working, with sentences made from what they recorded.
  • On a phone the tags on the right are hidden and the details open full width.
