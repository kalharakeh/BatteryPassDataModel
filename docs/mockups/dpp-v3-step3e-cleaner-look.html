Your cluster Cluster admins

  1. Add your people

    Under Cluster admin, Users: add someone as a user (sees your batteries) or as a cluster admin (also looks after users and tokens).

  2. Give your systems a token

    Under Cluster admin, API tokens: "Read" reads your batteries' passports; "Read + write" also sends their live values. Copy the value when it is shown: it is shown once.

  3. Check what they see

    Your users see your cluster's batteries in the Registry, with the purchaser values on the detail page.

From product data to a published passport Team

  1. Set up product data

    Families, models and batches, sites and operators. Most values are entered once and shared by every battery made from them.

  2. Create the battery

    Choose its batch, operator and cluster; type its serial number. It starts as a draft only the team sees.

  3. Fill in what is missing

    Every field says whether it is required by Feb 2027, required later or conditional. Only "by Feb 2027" blocks publishing.

  4. Publish

    One step checks, signs and publishes. Later changes ask first, then publish a new version; live values change it in place.

Status words Team

Draft
Not published yet; only the team sees it, on the Batteries tab.
Published
Its current version is public and signed.
Archived version
An earlier version, kept with its signature.

Before you call a battery ready Team

  • The model, batch, site and operator are complete.
  • The battery's own values and first live values are in.
  • Nothing blocks publication, and the battery says "Published".
  • The QR code opens the public summary.
