Team pages for the V3 passport — layout options

Mock-up for step 2d, the pages your team uses to enter product data, create packs, upload documents and publish passports. Nothing here is built yet. Each screen uses the real attribute names and the test pack (VPC8C05-6788). Where there's a choice, the options are side by side; the questions in yellow are what I need from you.

Shown on every field: the attribute number and name from the V3 file, the unit, who sees it (PublicPurchaserSharedAuthorities), the requirement (M, (M), C) and Feb 2027 when it's needed by then. A field turns red with the save pipeline's own reason when a value is refused.
