Battery pages — a simpler layout

Mock-up only; nothing is built yet. It uses the app's own stylesheets, so it looks like the app. site.css is not changed. Every card and action that works today stays; this changes where things are and how they look.

What's wrong today

  • On a phone the team's battery page is 8 to 11 screens long: nine cards stacked, six separate Save buttons. The old app showed one section at a time from a side menu, with one Save.
  • The battery status (#22) and the passport status (#2) look alike and live in different places. "Invalid" and its Undo are on the last card.
  • The header mixes facts, five plain links and notices, so it's hard to read and pushes the work down.
  • Bugs: no feedback while the demo data resets, the odd oval in the status window, no sign an accident report file was chosen, the wrong Compact 13M picture, two public search bugs. Each has a fix in the plan.

How to read this

  1. H: the new header and the two statuses. Every option uses it.
  2. A, B, C: three ways to lay out the rest of the page. One is built.
  3. F1–F6: how the cluster admin's page, the status window, the public page, the Registry, uploads and pictures follow.

Dashed outlines mark the "Upload a file" card from step 5c, which waits for this redesign.

H"What do the two statuses mean?" (the ? next to the badges)

One small window, the same on the team's, the cluster admin's and the public pages.

Two statuses

Battery (square tag) says what has happened to the battery. Passport (round pill) says whether this passport is the one that counts.

OriginalAs made; never repaired or reused.
Re-usedBack in use for its first purpose: after a repair (same passport), or prepared for re-use and placed on the market again (new passport).
RepurposedNow used for a different purpose. It gets a new passport.
RemanufacturedRebuilt to work as new. It gets a new passport.
WasteTaken out of use for recycling. Final; the team can then mark the passport for deletion.
ActiveThe passport that counts now.
ArchivedAn older version of this passport; a newer one counts.
InactiveReplaced by the battery's new passport after repurposing, remanufacturing or re-use elsewhere.
InvalidIts data shouldn't be trusted, for example after a market surveillance finding. Only the team undoes it.
Marked for deletionThe battery is waste and gone; the passport will be deleted.
DraftNot published yet; only the team sees it.
